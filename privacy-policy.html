Visagist Haircuts

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 28, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 8, 2026

We built Visagist Haircuts with your privacy in mind. We never store your photos. Images are processed in real time by AI and immediately discarded. This policy explains exactly what we collect and why.

1. Who We Are

Visagist Haircuts ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is an AI-powered haircut advisor application developed and operated by Oscar Oteiza. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@aurumnovasolution.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data needed to provide the App's features:

a) Account Information (via Google Sign-In)

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your display name
  • Your Google profile photo URL

This information is provided by Google when you choose to sign in and is used solely to identify your account within the App.

b) Usage Data

  • Number of AI analysis credits used
  • Timestamp of last usage
  • In-app purchase records (purchase date and product ID — no payment details)

This data is stored in Firebase Firestore under your user ID and is used to manage your credit balance and enforce usage limits.

c) Photos and Camera

The App requests access to your device camera and/or photo library to take or select a selfie for AI haircut analysis. Photos are sent directly to our AI processing provider for analysis and are never stored on our servers. Once the analysis is complete, the image is discarded. We do not retain, sell, or share your photos with any third party beyond the AI processing step.

d) Device Information

  • Device type and operating system version (collected automatically by Firebase)
  • App version
  • Anonymous installation ID

This is used for crash reporting and app performance monitoring.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To process your selfie through AI and return haircut recommendations
  • To track and enforce usage credits
  • To process and verify in-app purchases
  • To improve the App's performance and fix bugs

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sell it to any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database (Firestore), and analytics. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — account authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API (Google LLC) — AI image processing. Your selfie is transmitted to Google's Gemini API solely to generate haircut style previews. Images are not stored by Google beyond the duration of the API call. Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management and credit tracking. RevenueCat receives your app user ID and purchase transaction data to validate and manage your credit balance. No payment card details are shared with RevenueCat. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Play — in-app purchase processing. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play and never seen by us. Google Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage and Security

Your account data (email, name, usage counts) is stored in Google Firebase, which is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States. Firebase applies industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.

Photos are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections to our AI provider and are never written to any persistent storage.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or request deletion, we will remove your personal data from our systems within 30 days. Usage and purchase records may be retained for up to 90 days for fraud prevention purposes before being permanently deleted.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@aurumnovasolution.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. International Users

The App is available globally. By using the App, you consent to your data being processed in the United States where our service providers (Google Firebase, Google Gemini API, RevenueCat) operate. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify you through the App or via email. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
